
# Telugu Shorts Cartoon Characters

## Chintu
A cheerful 7-year-old cartoon boy.
Blue shirt, brown shorts, black hair.
Friendly expression and colorful 3D cartoon style.

## Bujji
A cute white cartoon rabbit.
Long ears, pink inner ears, big expressive eyes.
Friendly, playful and consistent in every scene.

## Visual style
Colorful children's 3D cartoon aesthetic.
Vertical 9:16 scenes.
Bright village backgrounds, soft lighting,
family-friendly stories and no scary imagery.
